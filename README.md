# TakeNotes

**A study notebook for iPad that quizzes you on what you wrote.**

Clickable prototype: https://blik.design/TakeNotes/

TakeNotes is a handwritten notebook for university students. One notebook per subject, pages by lecture date, and a weekly timetable that opens the right notebook during each class. What makes it different: the notebook turns your own notes into questions and brings back what you are about to forget.

## What the prototype shows

- **Onboarding** — one question per screen, daily questions, notifications, how questions are made.
- **Your week** — classes added once, repeating every week.
- **Home** — today’s class (“Open today’s page”), questions due today, notebooks, exams.
- **Editor** — paper switch in one tap (lines · grid · dots · blank), Materials panel for lecture PDFs, *Collect from pages* for definitions and formulas, lecture recording (Pro).
- **Question marks** — the app suggests questions from the structure of your notes (light Q); underline or circle to confirm (dark Q). Tap a Q to keep or remove it. No AI needed.
- **Test myself** — marked lines are hidden; recall, reveal, self-check.
- **Quiz types** — fill the gap (answer with Pencil), next step in a solution, name them all, term → definition, recall the formula.
- **Exam plan**, **lock-screen question**, **search in handwriting**, **plans**, **settings**.

Use **All screens** (top right) to jump to any screen, **Restart** to start over.

## Design principles

1. Study first, setup in one tap
2. Plain words, no jargon
3. One thing per screen
4. Hints in context, once
5. Calm, not gamified

## Notes
